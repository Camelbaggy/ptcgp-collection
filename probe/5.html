What We Provide

PocketMads is a community platform for tracking Pokémon TCG Pocket tournament results and competitive ranked match play. We provide:

  • Tournament result tracking and leaderboards
  • ELO-based ranked matchmaking
  • Player profiles with stats, ratings, and match history
  • A reputation system to encourage good sportsmanship

Fair Play & Conduct

The platform relies on an honor system for match result reporting. You agree to:

  • Report match results honestly and accurately
  • Check in for matches you've been matched to
  • Not intentionally manipulate your ELO rating (win-trading, throwing matches, etc.)
  • Not harass, abuse, or deliberately waste other players' time
  • Not exploit bugs or vulnerabilities in the platform

Violations are enforced through the reputation system. Players whose reputation falls below 40% are temporarily blocked from matchmaking. Severe or repeated violations may result in account suspension.

Match Results & Disputes

After a match, one player reports the result and the opponent has a window to confirm or dispute it. If the opponent does not respond before the deadline, the result is auto-confirmed.

Disputed results void the match — no ELO changes apply. Both players receive a reputation penalty for disputes. This system is designed to discourage both false reporting and frivolous disputes.
